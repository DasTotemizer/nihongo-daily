## ざるを{得|え}ない: have no choice but to

Take the ない form, replace ない with ざる, and add を{得|え}ない. する becomes せざる.

::: breakdown
ja: {雨|あめ} なので、{試合|しあい} を {中止|ちゅうし} せざる を {得|え}ない。
en: Because of the rain, we have no choice but to cancel the match.
- {中止|ちゅうし}せざる = not cancelling: the old negative
- を{得|え}ない = cannot obtain: there is no way to avoid it
:::

::: example
ja: {上司|じょうし} の {命令|めいれい} なら、{行|い}かざる を {得|え}ない。
en: If my boss orders it, I have no choice but to go.
:::

## やむを{得|え}ない: it can't be helped

::: example
ja: やむ を {得|え}ない {事情|じじょう} で {欠席|けっせき} します。
en: I will be absent for unavoidable reasons.
:::

## かねる: cannot, politely

The ます stem plus かねる means *unable to*. Staff use it to refuse politely.

::: example
ja: その {質問|しつもん} には お{答|こた}え しかねます。
en: I'm afraid I'm unable to answer that question.
:::

Be careful: かねない means the opposite. It means something *could well* happen, usually something bad.

::: example
ja: そんな {運転|うんてん} を したら、{事故|じこ} を {起|お}こしかねない。
en: Driving like that, you could easily cause an accident.
:::

::: callout
title: Mind the negative
かねる: I can't. かねない: it might well happen. One small ない flips the meaning.
:::
