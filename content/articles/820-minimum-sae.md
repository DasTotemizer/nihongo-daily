## さえ: even

さえ goes after a noun to say *even this*, the least you would expect.

::: example
ja: {忙|いそが}しくて、{食事|しょくじ} を する {時間|じかん} さえ ない。
en: I'm so busy I don't even have time to eat.
:::

::: example
ja: {子|こ}ども で さえ {知|し}って いる。
en: Even a child knows that.
:::

## さえ～ば: as long as

With a conditional, さえ means *if only* or *as long as*.

::: breakdown
ja: {時間|じかん} さえ あれば、{何|なん} でも できる。
en: As long as I have time, I can do anything.
- {時間|じかん}さえ = time, and only that
- あれば = if there is
- {何|なん}でもできる = can do anything
:::

::: example
ja: この {薬|くすり} を {飲|の}み さえ すれば、{治|なお}ります。
en: As long as you take this medicine, you'll get better.
:::

## すら: even, in writing

すら means the same as さえ in its *even* sense but sounds more formal.

::: example
ja: {彼|かれ} は {自分|じぶん} の {名前|なまえ} すら {書|か}けなかった。
en: He couldn't even write his own name.
:::

## おろか: let alone

A noun plus はおろか means *let alone*: the second, easier thing doesn't even happen.

::: example
ja: {漢字|かんじ} は おろか、ひらがな も {読|よ}めない。
en: I can't read hiragana, let alone kanji.
:::
