Here is a sentence that might look hard at first.

::: example
ja: {昨日|きのう} {駅|えき} の {近|ちか}く で {買|か}った パン は とても おいしかった。
en: The bread (I) bought near the station yesterday was very tasty.
:::

Let's take it apart in three steps.

## Step one: find the last verb or adjective

The main idea is at the end: おいしかった, "was tasty". Something was tasty.

## Step two: find what it is about

Look for は or が. Here it is パンは. So the bread was tasty.

## Step three: read what describes it

Everything before パン describes the bread. Split it at the particles.

::: breakdown
ja: {昨日|きのう} {駅|えき} の {近|ちか}く で {買|か}った パン
en: the bread (I) bought near the station yesterday
- {昨日|きのう} = yesterday
- {駅|えき}の{近|ちか}くで = near the station: where
- {買|か}った = bought
- パン = bread: the noun being described
:::

## Try another

::: example
ja: {毎朝|まいあさ} {犬|いぬ} と {公園|こうえん} を {歩|ある}く {人|ひと} が {多|おお}い。
en: Many people walk through the park with their dogs every morning.
:::

The end says {多|おお}い, "are many". What is many? {人|ひと}が, people. Which people? The ones who walk through the park with a dog every morning.

::: callout
title: The particle is the joint
Particles come after the word they mark. When you see one, the word just before it is finished. Draw a little line there in your head.
:::
