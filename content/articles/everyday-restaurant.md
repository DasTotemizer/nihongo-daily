## At the door

::: dialogue
{店員|てんいん}: いらっしゃいませ。{何名様|なんめいさま} です か。 // Welcome. How many people?
{客|きゃく}: {二人|ふたり} です。 // Two.
{店員|てんいん}: こちら へ どうぞ。 // This way, please.
:::

## Ordering

::: vocab
- すみません | excuse me | to call the staff
- メニュー を ください | the menu, please | request
- これ を {一|ひと}つ ください | one of these, please | pointing at the menu
- {私|わたし} は カレー に します | I'll have the curry | にする
- お{水|みず} を ください | some water, please | request
:::

::: example
ja: ビール を {二本|にほん} と、{焼|や}き{鳥|とり} を {五本|ごほん} ください。
en: Two beers and five skewers of yakitori, please.
:::

## Before and after eating

::: vocab
- いただきます | thank you for the food | before eating
- ごちそうさま でした | that was delicious | after eating
- お{会計|かいけい} お{願|ねが}いします | the bill, please | at the end
:::

::: callout
title: No tipping
Tipping is not part of Japanese restaurant culture. A sincere ごちそうさまでした on the way out is the thanks staff expect.
:::

::: quiz
? choice suru-naru
q: How do you order "I'll have the ramen"?
- ラーメン に します *
- ラーメン に なります
- ラーメン が します
why: Noun + にする is how you choose.

? choice numbers-counting
q: How do you say "two people" when asked how many?
- {二人|ふたり} *
- {二本|にほん}
- {二枚|にまい}
why: People are counted with {人|にん}, and two is the irregular {二人|ふたり}.
:::
