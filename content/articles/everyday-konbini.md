The till at a コンビニ follows a script. The staff speak very politely and quite fast, but the questions are always the same.

## What you will hear

::: vocab
- いらっしゃいませ | welcome | said as you enter
- {袋|ふくろ}は ご{利用|りよう}ですか | do you need a bag? | at the till
- {温|あたた}めますか | shall I heat it up? | for bento
- ポイントカードは ありますか | do you have a points card? | at the till
- お{箸|はし}は お{使|つか}いですか | do you need chopsticks? | for food
:::

You do not need to understand every polite word. Catch the key noun: {袋|ふくろ} (bag), {温|あたた}め (heat), カード (card), お{箸|はし} (chopsticks).

## What you can say

::: vocab
- はい、お{願|ねが}いします | yes, please | polite
- いいえ、{大丈夫|だいじょうぶ}です | no, I'm fine | polite
- カードで お{願|ねが}いします | by card, please | paying
:::

::: callout
title: The magic word
{大丈夫|だいじょうぶ}です means "I'm fine" and works as a polite no to almost any offer. You can add a small wave of the hand.
:::

## A whole exchange

::: dialogue
{店員|てんいん}: {温|あたた}めますか。 // Shall I heat it up?
{客|きゃく}: はい、お{願|ねが}いします。 // Yes, please.
{店員|てんいん}: {袋|ふくろ}は ご{利用|りよう}ですか。 // Do you need a bag?
{客|きゃく}: いいえ、{大丈夫|だいじょうぶ}です。 // No, I'm fine.
:::

::: quiz
? choice question-ka
q: The staff say {温|あたた}めますか. What are they asking?
- Shall I heat it up? *
- Do you need a bag?
- Is it hot outside?
why: {温|あたた}める means to heat up, and か makes it a question.

? choice polite-masu
q: How do you politely say no thanks?
- いいえ、{大丈夫|だいじょうぶ}です。 *
- いいえ、お{願|ねが}いします。
- はい、{大丈夫|だいじょうぶ}だ。
why: {大丈夫|だいじょうぶ}です is a polite "I'm fine".

? choice polite-masu
q: What does お{願|ねが}いします mean here?
- please *
- thank you
- excuse me
why: Use it to accept an offer, or to ask for something.
:::
