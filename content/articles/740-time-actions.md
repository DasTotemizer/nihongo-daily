## たばかり: have just done

A past verb plus ばかり means you have only just done it, and it still feels recent.

::: example
ja: {日本|にほん} に {来|き}た ばかり です。
en: I've only just come to Japan.
:::

::: example
ja: {買|か}った ばかり の スマホ が {壊|こわ}れた。
en: The phone I'd only just bought broke.
:::

## とたん: the moment that

A past verb plus とたん(に) means *as soon as*, often with something unexpected.

::: example
ja: {家|いえ} を {出|で}た とたん に {雨|あめ} が {降|ふ}り{出|だ}した。
en: The moment I left the house, it started to rain.
:::

## ながら: while

The ます stem plus ながら means doing two things at once. The main action comes last.

::: breakdown
ja: {音楽|おんがく} を {聞|き}きながら {勉強|べんきょう} する。
en: I study while listening to music.
- {音楽|おんがく}を{聞|き}きながら = while listening to music
- {勉強|べんきょう}する = I study: the main action
:::

ながら can also mean *although*.

::: example
ja: {悪|わる}い と {知|し}りながら、やって しまった。
en: Although I knew it was wrong, I did it anyway.
:::

## まくる: non-stop

The ます stem plus まくる is casual and means doing something like crazy.

::: example
ja: {週末|しゅうまつ} は ゲーム を やりまくった。
en: I played games non-stop all weekend.
:::
