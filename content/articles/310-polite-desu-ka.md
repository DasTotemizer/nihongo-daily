ます made verbs polite. For nouns and な-adjectives, the polite word is です. It replaces だ.

## だ becomes です

::: vocab
- {学生|がくせい}だ → {学生|がくせい}です | am a student | statement
- {学生|がくせい}じゃない → {学生|がくせい}じゃありません | am not a student | negative
- {学生|がくせい}だった → {学生|がくせい}でした | was a student | past
:::

::: breakdown
ja: {私|わたし} は {学生|がくせい} です。
en: I am a student.
- {私|わたし}は = as for me
- {学生|がくせい} = student
- です = polite "is"
:::

## い-adjectives: just add です

い-adjectives keep their own endings. です is added after, only for politeness.

::: example
ja: この ラーメン は おいしい です。
en: This ramen is delicious.
:::

::: example
ja: {昨日|きのう} は {寒|さむ}かった です。
en: It was cold yesterday.
note: The past is in {寒|さむ}かった. です only adds politeness.
:::

## か: making a question

Add か to the end of a polite sentence and it becomes a question. No change in word order is needed.

::: breakdown
ja: {学生|がくせい} です か。
en: Are (you) a student?
- {学生|がくせい}です = (you) are a student
- か = question marker
:::

::: example
ja: {日本語|にほんご} を {話|はな}します か。
en: Do you speak Japanese?
:::

::: example
ja: トイレ は どこ です か。
en: Where is the toilet?
:::

::: callout
title: No question mark needed
In careful writing, か is enough, and the sentence ends with 。 You will still see ？ in casual texts and manga.
:::

::: dialogue
A: {学生|がくせい} です か。 // Are you a student?
B: はい、{学生|がくせい} です。 // Yes, I'm a student.
A: {専攻|せんこう} は {何|なん} です か。 // What's your major?
B: {歴史|れきし} です。 // History.
:::

::: mistake
wrong: おいしい だ。
right: おいしい です。
why: い-adjectives never take だ, but they can take です for politeness.
:::

q: Which is correct for "It was cold"?
- {寒|さむ}かったです。 *
- {寒|さむ}いでした。
- {寒|さむ}いだった。
why: い-adjectives carry the past themselves. です only adds politeness.

? order question-ka
q: Put the words in order: "Where is the station?"
words: {駅|えき}は どこ です か
en: Where is the station?
why: Topic first, then the question word, then です and か at the end.
