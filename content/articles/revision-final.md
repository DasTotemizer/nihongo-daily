## Advanced patterns at a glance

::: vocab
- {子|こ}どもでさえ | even a child | minimum
- {行|い}かざるを{得|え}ない | have no choice but to go | no choice
- {忘|わす}れがち | tend to forget | tendency
- {泥|どろ}まみれ | covered in mud | covered in
- {見|み}るや{否|いな}や | the moment someone saw | proximity
- {迷|まよ}ったあげく | after much hesitation | in the end
:::

## A short tale using them all

::: example
ja: {雨|あめ} の {日|ひ}、{犬|いぬ} は {外|そと} に {出|で}る や {否|いな}や {泥|どろ} まみれ に なった。
en: On a rainy day, the moment the dog went outside, it got covered in mud.
:::

::: example
ja: {洗|あら}う そば から また {汚|よご}れる ので、{散々|さんざん} {迷|まよ}った あげく、{今日|きょう} は {家|いえ} で {遊|あそ}ばせる こと に した。
en: It kept getting dirty again as soon as I washed it, so after much thought I decided to let it play at home today.
:::
