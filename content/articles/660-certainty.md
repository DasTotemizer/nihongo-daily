## かもしれない: maybe

Add かもしれない after a plain verb or adjective, or straight after a noun or な-adjective. Casually, it shortens to かも.

::: example
ja: {明日|あした} は {雨|あめ} かもしれない。
en: It might rain tomorrow.
:::

::: example
ja: {彼|かれ} は もう {帰|かえ}った かも。
en: He might have gone home already.
:::

## でしょう: probably

でしょう sounds polite and fairly confident. It is the weather forecaster's favourite word.

::: example
ja: {午後|ごご} は {晴|は}れる でしょう。
en: It will probably be sunny in the afternoon.
:::

With a rising tone, でしょう? checks that the listener agrees, like *right?*

::: example
ja: これ、おいしい でしょう?
en: This is good, right?
:::

## だろう: probably, plainly

だろう is the casual version. It often appears in thoughts and in writing.

::: example
ja: {彼女|かのじょ} は {来|こ}ない だろう と {思|おも}う。
en: I think she probably won't come.
:::

::: callout
title: Shades of certainty
From least to most certain: かもしれない, でしょう or だろう, then a plain statement with no hedge at all.
:::
