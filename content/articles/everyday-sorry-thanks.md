Japanese has several ways to say thank you and sorry, from casual to very formal. Here are the ones you will use most.

## Thank you

::: vocab
- ありがとう | thanks | casual
- ありがとう ございます | thank you | polite
- どうも | thanks | short, casual
- どうも ありがとう ございます | thank you very much | very polite
:::

When someone thanks you, you can reply どういたしまして (you're welcome), though people often just smile or say いいえ (not at all).

## Sorry and excuse me

::: vocab
- ごめんなさい | I'm sorry | personal apology
- ごめん | sorry | casual
- すみません | excuse me, sorry | all-purpose
- {失礼|しつれい}します | excuse me | entering or leaving a room
:::

::: callout
title: The all-purpose word
すみません can mean excuse me, sorry, and even thank you, when someone has gone to trouble for you. If in doubt, say すみません.
:::

::: example
ja: すみません、ちょっと いい です か。
en: Excuse me, do you have a moment?
:::

::: example
ja: {遅|おく}れて ごめんなさい。
en: Sorry I'm late.
:::
