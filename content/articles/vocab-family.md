When you talk about your own family to others, you use humble words. When you talk about someone else's family, or speak to your own family members, you use respectful ones.

## Your own family

::: vocab
- {家族|かぞく} | family | noun
- {父|ちち} | my father | humble
- {母|はは} | my mother | humble
- {兄|あに} | my older brother | humble
- {姉|あね} | my older sister | humble
- {弟|おとうと} | my younger brother | humble
- {妹|いもうと} | my younger sister | humble
:::

## Someone else's family

::: vocab
- お{父|とう}さん | father | respectful
- お{母|かあ}さん | mother | respectful
- お{兄|にい}さん | older brother | respectful
- お{姉|ねえ}さん | older sister | respectful
:::

::: callout
title: At home, use the respectful set
Children call their parents お{母|かあ}さん and お{父|とう}さん. The humble words are for talking about them to outsiders.
:::

## Talking about family

::: example
ja: {母|はは} は {先生|せんせい} だ。
en: My mother is a teacher.
:::

::: example
ja: お{母|かあ}さん は {元気|げんき}？
en: How is your mother?
:::

Older and younger matter in Japanese. There is no single word for "brother" in everyday use: you say which one.

::: quiz
? choice state-of-being
q: How do you say "My mother is a teacher" to a friend?
- {母|はは}は{先生|せんせい}だ。 *
- お{母|かあ}さんは{先生|せんせい}だ。
why: For your own mother, talking to an outsider, use the humble {母|はは}.

? choice particle-wa
q: How do you ask about a friend's father?
- お{父|とう}さんは？ *
- {父|ちち}は？
why: Someone else's family takes the respectful set.

? match particle-wa
q: Match each word to its meaning.
- {兄|あに} = my older brother
- {弟|おとうと} = my younger brother
- {姉|あね} = my older sister
- {妹|いもうと} = my younger sister
why: Japanese always says whether a sibling is older or younger.
:::
