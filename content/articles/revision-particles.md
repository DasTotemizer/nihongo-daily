Particles tell you each word's job in a sentence. Here are the ones you have learned, each with its job.

## The chart

::: vocab
- は | the topic: as for | particle
- が | the subject, new information | particle
- を | the object, what is acted on | particle
- に | a destination, a time, where something is | particle
- へ | a direction, said e | particle
- で | where an action happens, or the means | particle
:::

## One sentence, many particles

::: breakdown
ja: {私|わたし} は {日曜日|にちようび} に {友|とも}だち と {公園|こうえん} で お{弁当|べんとう} を {食|た}べた。
en: On Sunday, I ate a packed lunch in the park with a friend.
- {私|わたし}は = as for me: the topic
- {日曜日|にちようび}に = on Sunday: the time
- {友|とも}だちと = with a friend
- {公園|こうえん}で = in the park: where it happened
- お{弁当|べんとう}を = a packed lunch: the object
- {食|た}べた = ate
:::

Each particle comes right after the word it marks. Because of that, you can move the pieces around and the meaning stays the same. Only the verb has to stay at the end.
