## やすい: easy to do

::: example
ja: この ペン は {書|か}きやすい。
en: This pen is easy to write with.
:::

::: example
ja: {先生|せんせい} の {説明|せつめい} は わかりやすい。
en: The teacher's explanations are easy to understand.
:::

## にくい: hard to do

::: example
ja: この {肉|にく} は {硬|かた}くて {食|た}べにくい。
en: This meat is tough and hard to eat.
:::

::: example
ja: {言|い}いにくい ん だ けど……
en: This is hard to say, but...
:::

## They work like い-adjectives

::: vocab
- {読|よ}みやすい | easy to read | present
- {読|よ}みやすくない | not easy to read | negative
- {読|よ}みやすかった | was easy to read | past
- {読|よ}みやすくて | easy to read, and | て form
:::

::: callout
title: づらい and がたい
づらい also means *hard to do*, with a feeling of discomfort: {歩|ある}きづらい, awkward to walk in. がたい is a formal written version: {信|しん}じがたい, hard to believe.
:::

::: mistake
wrong: {読|よ}むやすい
right: {読|よ}みやすい
why: やすい joins the ます stem, not the dictionary form.
:::
