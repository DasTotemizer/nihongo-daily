These are the verbs of an ordinary day. The group, る-verb or う-verb, is marked for each, because every form you make depends on it.

## Morning

::: vocab
- {起|お}きる | get up | る-verb
- {顔|かお}を{洗|あら}う | wash (your) face | う-verb
- {食|た}べる | eat | る-verb
- {出|で}かける | go out | る-verb
:::

## Daytime

::: vocab
- {行|い}く | go | う-verb
- {働|はたら}く | work | う-verb
- {勉強|べんきょう}する | study | irregular
- {会|あ}う | meet | う-verb
:::

## Evening

::: vocab
- {帰|かえ}る | go home | う-verb
- {作|つく}る | make, cook | う-verb
- {見|み}る | watch | る-verb
- {寝|ね}る | sleep | る-verb
:::

## A day, told in the past

::: example
ja: {七時|しちじ} に {起|お}きた。
en: (I) got up at seven.
:::

::: example
ja: {友|とも}だち に {会|あ}った。
en: (I) met a friend.
note: {会|あ}う marks the person with に, not を.
:::

::: example
ja: {十一時|じゅういちじ} に {寝|ね}た。
en: (I) went to bed at eleven.
:::
