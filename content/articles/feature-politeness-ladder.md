## Is the teacher here?

::: vocab
- {先生|せんせい}、いる? | is the teacher in? | casual, to a friend
- {先生|せんせい} は います か | is the teacher in? | polite
- {先生|せんせい} は いらっしゃいます か | is the teacher in? | honorific
:::

## I'll go

::: vocab
- {行|い}く よ | I'll go | casual
- {行|い}きます | I'll go | polite
- {参|まい}ります | I'll go | humble
:::

## Please wait

::: vocab
- {待|ま}って | wait | casual
- {待|ま}って ください | please wait | polite
- お{待|ま}ち ください | please wait | honorific
- {少々|しょうしょう} お{待|ま}ち いただけます か | could you wait a moment? | very polite
:::

::: callout
title: Up for them, down for you
Honorific words raise the other person's actions. Humble words lower your own. Ordinary です and ます sit in between and are always safe.
:::

::: quiz
? choice honorific
q: Which is the honorific way to ask if the manager is in?
- {部長|ぶちょう} は いらっしゃいます か *
- {部長|ぶちょう} は おります か
- {部長|ぶちょう}、いる?
why: いらっしゃる raises the manager. おる is humble, for your own side.

? choice humble
q: Which is humble?
- {参|まい}ります *
- いらっしゃいます
- {行|い}く よ
why: {参|まい}る lowers your own action.
:::
