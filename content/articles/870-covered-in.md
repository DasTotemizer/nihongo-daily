## だらけ: full of, everywhere

Add だらけ to a noun. It usually sounds negative.

::: example
ja: この {作文|さくぶん} は {間違|まちが}い だらけ だ。
en: This essay is full of mistakes.
:::

::: example
ja: {部屋|へや} が ゴミ だらけ に なった。
en: The room ended up covered in rubbish.
:::

## まみれ: smeared with

まみれ is for something sticking to the whole surface, such as mud, sweat or blood.

::: example
ja: {子|こ}ども たち は {泥|どろ} まみれ に なって {遊|あそ}んだ。
en: The children played until they were covered in mud.
:::

::: example
ja: {汗|あせ} まみれ の シャツ
en: a shirt soaked with sweat
:::

## ずくめ: nothing but

ずくめ means *entirely made of*, and it can be positive.

::: example
ja: {今年|ことし} は いい こと ずくめ だった。
en: This year was nothing but good things.
:::

::: example
ja: {黒|くろ} ずくめ の {服|ふく}
en: an outfit all in black
:::

::: callout
title: Choosing one
だらけ: lots of something scattered everywhere. まみれ: a sticky layer all over. ずくめ: completely made up of one thing.
:::
