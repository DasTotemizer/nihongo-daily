## One sentence, six shades

::: vocab
- {雨|あめ}が{降|ふ}りそう | looks like it's about to rain | from what I see right now
- {雨|あめ}が{降|ふ}る そうだ | I hear it will rain | from what I was told
- {雨|あめ}が{降|ふ}った ようだ | it seems it rained | from evidence
- {雨|あめ}が{降|ふ}った みたい | seems it rained | casual よう
- {雨|あめ}が{降|ふ}る らしい | apparently it will rain | from what's going around
- {雨|あめ}が{降|ふ}る かもしれない | it might rain | a guess
:::

::: callout
title: The key question
Ask yourself where the idea came from. Your own eyes: そう. Evidence you worked out: よう or みたい. Someone told you: そうだ or らしい.
:::

::: example
ja: {道|みち} が {濡|ぬ}れて いる。{夜中|よなか} に {雨|あめ} が {降|ふ}った よう だ。
en: The road is wet. It seems it rained during the night.
:::

::: quiz
? choice seems-like
q: Looking at dark clouds, you say:
- {雨|あめ} が {降|ふ}りそう だ *
- {雨|あめ} が {降|ふ}る そう だ
- {雨|あめ} が {降|ふ}った らしい
why: You're judging from what you see right now: stem plus そう.

? choice hearsay
q: You read it in the news. Which fits?
- {雨|あめ} が {降|ふ}る そう だ *
- {雨|あめ} が {降|ふ}りそう だ
- {雨|あめ} が {降|ふ}ろう
why: The full form plus そうだ passes on what you heard or read.
:::
