This story is invented, but it is written in real newspaper style. Read each sentence, then check the breakdown.

::: breakdown
ja: {市|し} の {調査|ちょうさ} に よる と、{市内|しない} の {自転車|じてんしゃ} {利用者|りようしゃ} は {増|ふ}えつつ ある。
en: According to a city survey, the number of cyclists in the city is rising.
- {調査|ちょうさ}によると = according to the survey
- {利用者|りようしゃ} = users
- {増|ふ}えつつある = is steadily increasing
:::

::: breakdown
ja: {特|とく} に {若者|わかもの} の {間|あいだ} で {人気|にんき} が {高|たか}く、{通勤|つうきん} に {使|つか}う {人|ひと} も {多|おお}い。
en: It is especially popular among young people, and many use bikes to commute.
- {特|とく}に = especially
- {若者|わかもの}の{間|あいだ}で = among young people
- {通勤|つうきん} = commuting
:::

::: breakdown
ja: {一方|いっぽう}、{事故|じこ} も {増|ふ}える {傾向|けいこう} に あり、{市|し} は {対策|たいさく} を {急|いそ}ぐ べき で ある。
en: On the other hand, accidents are also on the rise, and the city ought to act quickly.
- {一方|いっぽう} = on the other hand
- {傾向|けいこう}にあり = there is a tendency, and
- {急|いそ}ぐべきである = ought to hurry: formal
:::

::: callout
title: How to read the news
Find the verb at the end of each sentence first. Then work back to see who is doing it. Formal writing joins long clauses with the ます stem, such as あり.
:::
