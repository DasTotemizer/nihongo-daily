"It's hot, isn't it?" is one of the most common things people say in Japan, especially in summer. Weather talk is how strangers and neighbours say hello.

## The seasons

::: vocab
- {春|はる} | spring | noun
- {夏|なつ} | summer | noun
- {秋|あき} | autumn | noun
- {冬|ふゆ} | winter | noun
:::

## The weather

::: vocab
- {天気|てんき} | weather | noun
- {晴|は}れ | sunny | noun
- {雨|あめ} | rain | noun
- {雪|ゆき} | snow | noun
- {風|かぜ} | wind | noun
- {暑|あつ}い | hot (weather) | い-adjective
- {寒|さむ}い | cold (weather) | い-adjective
- {暖|あたた}かい | warm | い-adjective
- {涼|すず}しい | cool | い-adjective
:::

::: callout
title: Hot weather, hot tea
{暑|あつ}い is hot weather. A hot drink uses a different kanji, {熱|あつ}い, with the same sound. And {冷|つめ}たい is cold to the touch, not cold weather.
:::

## Small talk

::: example
ja: {今日|きょう} は {暑|あつ}い ね。
en: It's hot today, isn't it?
:::

::: example
ja: {明日|あした} は {雨|あめ} だ よ。
en: It's going to rain tomorrow, you know.
:::

::: dialogue
A: {寒|さむ}い ね。 // Cold, isn't it?
B: そう だ ね。{雪|ゆき} かな。 // It is. Maybe snow.
:::

::: quiz
? choice i-adjectives
q: How do you say "It's cold" (the weather)?
- {寒|さむ}い。 *
- {冷|つめ}たい。
- {暑|あつ}い。
why: {寒|さむ}い is for cold weather. {冷|つめ}たい is cold to touch.

? choice ending-ne-yo
q: You want your neighbour to agree that it's warm. What do you say?
- {暖|あたた}かいね。 *
- {暖|あたた}かいよ。
why: ね invites agreement.

? match i-adjectives
q: Match each season to its meaning.
- {春|はる} = spring
- {夏|なつ} = summer
- {秋|あき} = autumn
- {冬|ふゆ} = winter
why: Japan's four seasons, in order.
:::
