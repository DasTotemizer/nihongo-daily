Food is one of the best ways into a language, because you meet the words every day. Notice how many are in katakana: they came from other languages.

## The basics

::: vocab
- ごはん | rice, a meal | noun
- {水|みず} | water | noun
- お{茶|ちゃ} | tea, especially green tea | noun
- {魚|さかな} | fish | noun
- {肉|にく} | meat | noun
- {野菜|やさい} | vegetables | noun
- {卵|たまご} | egg | noun
- {果物|くだもの} | fruit | noun
:::

ごはん means cooked rice, but it also means a meal. あさごはん is breakfast: morning rice.

## Borrowed words

::: vocab
- パン | bread | from Portuguese
- コーヒー | coffee | from English
- ジュース | juice | from English
- ミルク | milk | from English
- ラーメン | ramen | from Chinese
- カレー | curry | from English
- サラダ | salad | from English
- ケーキ | cake | from English
:::

::: callout
title: Read the long dash
The ー in コーヒー and ケーキ makes the sound before it longer. ケーキ is kee-ki, not ke-ki.
:::

## Saying what you like

::: example
ja: ラーメン が {好|す}き。
en: (I) like ramen.
:::

::: example
ja: いただきます。
en: (Said before eating.) Let's eat.
note: Literally "I humbly receive". People say it before every meal.
:::
