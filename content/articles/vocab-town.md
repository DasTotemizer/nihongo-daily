Most places in a town have short, practical names. Several end in the same kanji, which helps a lot.

## Places

::: vocab
- {駅|えき} | station | noun
- {銀行|ぎんこう} | bank | noun
- {病院|びょういん} | hospital | noun
- {郵便局|ゆうびんきょく} | post office | noun
- {図書館|としょかん} | library | noun
- {公園|こうえん} | park | noun
- {学校|がっこう} | school | noun
- スーパー | supermarket | noun
- コンビニ | convenience store | noun
:::

::: callout
title: Building blocks
{館|かん} means a building for something, as in {図書館|としょかん} (library) and {映画館|えいがかん} (cinema). {院|いん} appears in places like {病院|びょういん} (hospital).
:::

## Going there, doing things there

::: example
ja: {銀行|ぎんこう} に {行|い}く。
en: (I'm) going to the bank.
:::

::: example
ja: スーパー で {野菜|やさい} を {買|か}う。
en: (I) buy vegetables at the supermarket.
:::

::: example
ja: {駅|えき} の {前|まえ} に コンビニ が ある。
en: There is a convenience store in front of the station.
:::

::: quiz
? choice particle-ni-he
q: Fill the gap: {病院|びょういん} ＿ {行|い}く。 (go to the hospital)
- に *
- で
why: The destination takes に.

? choice particle-de
q: Fill the gap: {公園|こうえん} ＿ {遊|あそ}ぶ。 (play in the park)
- で *
- に
why: Playing is an action that happens there, so で.

? match particle-ni-he
q: Match each place to its meaning.
- {駅|えき} = station
- {銀行|ぎんこう} = bank
- {図書館|としょかん} = library
- {郵便局|ゆうびんきょく} = post office
why: These are the town places you will see on signs every day.
:::
