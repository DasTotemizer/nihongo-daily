## One to ten

::: vocab
- {一|いち} | one | 1
- {二|に} | two | 2
- {三|さん} | three | 3
- {四|よん} | four | 4, also し
- {五|ご} | five | 5
- {六|ろく} | six | 6
- {七|なな} | seven | 7, also しち
- {八|はち} | eight | 8
- {九|きゅう} | nine | 9, also く
- {十|じゅう} | ten | 10
:::

Bigger numbers are built like a sum: {十一|じゅういち} is 10 and 1, {二十|にじゅう} is 2 tens, {三十五|さんじゅうご} is 35.

::: vocab
- {百|ひゃく} | a hundred | 100
- {千|せん} | a thousand | 1,000
- {万|まん} | ten thousand | 10,000
:::

::: callout
title: Counting in ten thousands
Japanese groups big numbers in fours, not threes. A million is {百万|ひゃくまん}, a hundred ten-thousands.
:::

## Counters

You can't just say *three books*. You need a counter between the number and what it counts.

::: vocab
- {一枚|いちまい} | one flat thing | tickets, paper, shirts
- {一本|いっぽん} | one long thing | bottles, pens, trains
- {一匹|いっぴき} | one small animal | cats, fish
- {一冊|いっさつ} | one book | books, magazines
- {一人|ひとり} | one person | {二人|ふたり} is two people
- {一つ|ひとつ} | one thing | general counter, up to ten
:::

::: breakdown
ja: ビール を {二本|にほん} ください。
en: Two beers, please.
- ビールを = beer
- {二本|にほん} = two long things: bottles
- ください = please
:::

::: example
ja: {猫|ねこ} が {三匹|さんびき} いる。
en: There are three cats.
:::

The sounds often change: {一本|いっぽん}, {三本|さんぼん}, {六本|ろっぽん}. Learn these as you meet them.

## Dates and times

::: vocab
- {一時|いちじ} | one o'clock | 時 for hours
- {五分|ごふん} | five minutes | 分 for minutes
- {一月|いちがつ} | January | months are just numbers
- {一日|ついたち} | the first of the month | irregular
- {何時|なんじ} | what time | question
:::

q: How do you say 25?
- {二十五|にじゅうご} *
- {五二十|ごにじゅう}
- {二五|にご}
why: Two tens and five.

? choice numbers-counting
q: Which counter would you use for pencils?
- {本|ほん} *
- {枚|まい}
- {匹|ひき}
why: {本|ほん} counts long, thin things.

? match numbers-counting
q: Match each counter to what it counts.
- {枚|まい} = flat things
- {冊|さつ} = books
- {匹|ひき} = small animals
- {人|にん} = people
why: The counter tells the listener what kind of thing you are counting.
:::
