## てみる: try it and see

The て form plus みる (see) means *do it and see what happens*.

::: example
ja: この {服|ふく} を {着|き}て みて も いい です か。
en: May I try this on?
:::

::: example
ja: {納豆|なっとう} を {食|た}べて みた けど、ちょっと {苦手|にがて} だった。
en: I tried natto, but I didn't really like it.
:::

::: callout
title: Written in hiragana
Because みる here doesn't mean *see* with your eyes, it is written in hiragana, not {見|み}る.
:::

## ようとする: attempt to do

The volitional plus とする means *try to* or *be about to*. It often comes before a sentence saying it didn't work.

::: breakdown
ja: {寝|ね}よう と した けど、{寝|ね}られなかった。
en: I tried to sleep, but I couldn't.
- {寝|ね}ようとした = tried to sleep
- けど = but
- {寝|ね}られなかった = couldn't sleep
:::

::: example
ja: {家|いえ} を {出|で}よう と した とき、{電話|でんわ} が {鳴|な}った。
en: Just as I was about to leave the house, the phone rang.
:::

## ようにする: make an effort to

::: example
ja: {毎日|まいにち} {野菜|やさい} を {食|た}べる よう に して いる。
en: I make a point of eating vegetables every day.
:::
