Once you can make the て form, a whole family of patterns opens up. Each one adds a small word after it.

## The family

::: vocab
- {食|た}べて、{寝|ね}た | ate, and then slept | joining
- {食|た}べて ください | please eat | request
- {食|た}べて いる | is eating | ongoing
- {作|つく}って ある | has been made | done on purpose
- {買|か}って おく | buy in advance | preparation
- {持|も}って いく | take along | direction
- {食|た}べて みる | try eating | trying
- {食|た}べて も いい | may eat | permission
- {食|た}べて は いけない | must not eat | prohibition
:::

## One morning, many patterns

::: breakdown
ja: {朝|あさ} {起|お}きて、{顔|かお} を {洗|あら}って、{昨日|きのう} {買|か}って おいた パン を {食|た}べて いる。
en: I got up, washed my face, and now I'm eating the bread I bought yesterday.
- {起|お}きて = got up, and
- {洗|あら}って = washed, and
- {買|か}っておいた = bought in advance
- {食|た}べている = am eating
:::

::: quiz
? choice te-aru-oku
q: Which means "I'll buy it in advance"?
- {買|か}って おく *
- {買|か}って いる
- {買|か}って みる
why: ておく is for preparation.

? match te-iru
q: Match each pattern to its meaning.
- {寝|ね}て いる = is sleeping
- {寝|ね}て みる = try sleeping
- {寝|ね}て も いい = may sleep
- {寝|ね}て ください = please sleep
why: The word after the て form decides the meaning.
:::
