## The casual volitional

- る-verbs: change る to よう. {食|た}べる becomes {食|た}べよう.
- う-verbs: move to the *o* column and add う. {行|い}く becomes {行|い}こう.
- する becomes しよう, and {来|く}る becomes {来|こ}よう.

::: vocab
- {見|み}よう | let's watch | る-verb
- {飲|の}もう | let's drink | む → もう
- {帰|かえ}ろう | let's go home | る → ろう
- {待|ま}とう | let's wait | つ → とう
- しよう | let's do it | irregular
:::

::: example
ja: {一緒|いっしょ} に {帰|かえ}ろう。
en: Let's go home together.
:::

## The polite volitional

Use the ます stem plus ましょう. Add か to make an offer or a gentle question.

::: example
ja: {少|すこ}し {休|やす}みましょう。
en: Let's take a short break.
:::

::: example
ja: {荷物|にもつ} を {持|も}ちましょう か。
en: Shall I carry your bag?
:::

## Making suggestions

Conditionals with いい suggest what would be good to do. どう asks *how about*.

::: example
ja: {先生|せんせい} に {聞|き}けば いい よ。
en: You should just ask the teacher.
:::

::: example
ja: {少|すこ}し {寝|ね}たら どう?
en: Why don't you get some sleep?
:::

::: callout
title: Let's, or shall we?
ましょう suggests doing something together. ましょうか offers to do it, or asks whether you should.
:::

? choice volitional
q: What is the casual volitional of {泳|およ}ぐ?
- {泳|およ}ごう *
- {泳|およ}ぎよう
- {泳|およ}げよう
why: う-verbs move to the o column and add う.

? choice volitional
q: How do you offer "Shall I open the window?"
- {窓|まど} を {開|あ}けましょう か *
- {窓|まど} を {開|あ}けたい です
- {窓|まど} を {開|あ}けて は いけません
why: ましょうか offers to do something.

? choice volitional
q: Which is a suggestion: "Why don't you go to the doctor?"
- {病院|びょういん} に {行|い}ったら どう *
- {病院|びょういん} に {行|い}こう
- {病院|びょういん} に {行|い}かなきゃ
why: たらどう asks *how about doing it?*
:::
