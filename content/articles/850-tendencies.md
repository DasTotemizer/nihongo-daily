## がち: tends to

Add がち to a verb stem or a noun. It usually describes something undesirable.

::: example
ja: {冬|ふゆ} は {風邪|かぜ} を ひきがち だ。
en: I tend to catch colds in winter.
:::

::: example
ja: {彼|かれ} は {病気|びょうき}がち な {子|こ} だった。
en: He was a sickly child.
:::

::: example
ja: {最近|さいきん}、{曇|くも}りがち の {天気|てんき} が {続|つづ}いて いる。
en: Lately the weather has tended to be cloudy.
:::

## つつ: while, and still in progress

The ます stem plus つつ is a formal *while*, like ながら. It can also mean *although*.

::: example
ja: {体|からだ} に {悪|わる}い と {知|し}りつつ、タバコ を やめられない。
en: Although I know it's bad for me, I can't quit smoking.
:::

つつある means something is in the middle of changing.

::: example
ja: {地球|ちきゅう} の {気温|きおん} は {上|あ}がりつつ ある。
en: The Earth's temperature is rising.
:::

## きらいがある: an unfortunate tendency

きらい here doesn't mean *dislike*. It describes a bad habit. It is formal and often used in criticism.

::: example
ja: {彼|かれ} は {物事|ものごと} を {大|おお}げさ に {言|い}う きらい が ある。
en: He has a tendency to exaggerate things.
:::
