## ば: the general if

To make the ば form:

- Verbs: change the last sound to the *e* column and add ば. {行|い}く becomes {行|い}けば; {食|た}べる becomes {食|た}べれば.
- い-adjectives: change い to ければ. {安|やす}い becomes {安|やす}ければ.
- Negatives work like い-adjectives: ない becomes なければ.
- Nouns and な-adjectives use であれば, or just なら.

::: example
ja: {安|やす}ければ、{買|か}う。
en: If it's cheap, I'll buy it.
:::

::: example
ja: {急|いそ}げば、{間|ま}に{合|あ}う。
en: If you hurry, you'll make it.
:::

## たら: if, or when

To make the たら form, take the past tense and add ら. It works for every kind of word.

::: vocab
- {行|い}った → {行|い}ったら | if or when I go | verb
- {高|たか}かった → {高|たか}かったら | if it's expensive | い-adjective
- {雨|あめ}だった → {雨|あめ}だったら | if it rains | noun
:::

::: breakdown
ja: {家|いえ} に {帰|かえ}ったら、{電話|でんわ} する ね。
en: When I get home, I'll call you.
- {家|いえ}に = home
- {帰|かえ}ったら = when I get back
- {電話|でんわ}するね = I'll call, OK?
:::

たら can also describe a surprise discovery in the past.

::: example
ja: ドア を {開|あ}けたら、{猫|ねこ} が いた。
en: When I opened the door, there was a cat.
:::

::: callout
title: Choosing a conditional
と: automatic results. なら: if that's the case. ば: general conditions. たら: most other cases, including plans and requests.
:::

q: What is the ば form of {見|み}る?
- {見|み}れば *
- {見|み}えば
- {見|み}たば
why: る-verbs change る to れば.

? choice conditional-ba-tara
q: How do you say "if it's not expensive"?
- {高|たか}くなければ *
- {高|たか}いなければ
- {高|たか}くないば
why: ない changes to なければ, just like an い-adjective.

? order conditional-ba-tara
q: Put the words in order: "When I arrive, I'll send an email."
words: {着|つ}いたら メール を {送|おく}る
en: When I arrive, I'll send an email.
why: たら sets the condition, and the plan follows.
