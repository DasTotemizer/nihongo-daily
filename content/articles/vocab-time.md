Japanese days of the week all end in {曜日|ようび}, "day of the week". What comes before is one simple kanji.

## The week

::: vocab
- {月曜日|げつようび} | Monday | moon
- {火曜日|かようび} | Tuesday | fire
- {水曜日|すいようび} | Wednesday | water
- {木曜日|もくようび} | Thursday | wood, tree
- {金曜日|きんようび} | Friday | metal, gold
- {土曜日|どようび} | Saturday | earth
- {日曜日|にちようび} | Sunday | sun
:::

Monday is the moon's day in English too, and Sunday is the sun's. The pattern matches.

## Around today

::: vocab
- {今日|きょう} | today | noun
- {明日|あした} | tomorrow | noun
- {昨日|きのう} | yesterday | noun
- {毎日|まいにち} | every day | noun
- {今週|こんしゅう} | this week | noun
- {週末|しゅうまつ} | weekend | noun
:::

::: callout
title: Special readings
{今日|きょう}, {明日|あした} and {昨日|きのう} are read as whole words. You cannot work out their sounds from the single kanji. Learn them as units.
:::

## In a sentence

::: example
ja: {金曜日|きんようび} に {映画|えいが} を {見|み}る。
en: (I'll) watch a film on Friday.
note: Days of the week take に. Today and tomorrow do not.
:::

::: example
ja: {今日|きょう} は {土曜日|どようび} だ。
en: Today is Saturday.
:::
