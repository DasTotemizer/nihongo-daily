## まま: unchanged

まま is a noun meaning *as it is*. Attach it like any noun: after a past verb, after い-adjectives directly, and after nouns with の.

::: example
ja: {電気|でんき} を つけた まま {寝|ね}て しまった。
en: I fell asleep with the light on.
:::

::: example
ja: {靴|くつ} を はいた まま {入|はい}らないで ください。
en: Please don't come in with your shoes on.
:::

::: example
ja: この まま で いい です。
en: It's fine as it is.
:::

::: example
ja: {昔|むかし} の まま だ。
en: It's just as it was long ago.
:::

## っぱなし: left that way, carelessly

The ます stem plus っぱなし means something has been left in a state when it shouldn't have been.

::: example
ja: {窓|まど} を {開|あ}けっぱなし に しないで。
en: Don't leave the window open.
:::

::: example
ja: {一日|いちにち} {中|じゅう} {立|た}ちっぱなし だった。
en: I was on my feet all day.
:::

::: callout
title: まま or っぱなし?
まま is neutral. っぱなし suggests a state that went on too long or was forgotten.
:::
