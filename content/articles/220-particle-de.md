The particle で marks the context of an action: where it takes place, or the means used to do it. If に is the target, で is the stage.

## Where an action happens

::: breakdown
ja: {図書館|としょかん} で {勉強|べんきょう} する。
en: (I) study at the library.
- {図書館|としょかん} = library
- で = the place where the action happens
- {勉強|べんきょう}する = study
:::

::: example
ja: {駅|えき} で {友|とも}だち を {待|ま}った。
en: (I) waited for a friend at the station.
:::

## に or で?

This is the classic question. Both can come after a place, but they mean different things.

- に marks where something **exists** or where you **go**: {部屋|へや}にいる (be in the room), {部屋|へや}に{入|はい}る (go into the room).
- で marks where an **action happens**: {部屋|へや}で{寝|ね}る (sleep in the room).

::: mistake
wrong: {図書館|としょかん} に {勉強|べんきょう} する。
right: {図書館|としょかん} で {勉強|べんきょう} する。
why: Studying is an action that happens at the library, so the place takes で.
:::

## How you do it

で also marks the tool or method.

::: example
ja: バス で {行|い}く。
en: (I'll) go by bus.
:::

::: example
ja: {日本語|にほんご} で {話|はな}す。
en: (We) speak in Japanese.
:::

::: example
ja: はし で {食|た}べる。
en: (I) eat with chopsticks.
:::

## What is the place for? なにで vs なんで

Asking なにで means "by what means". You may also hear なんで, which more often means "why". Context makes it clear, but it is a fun pair to notice.

::: quiz
? choice particle-de
q: Fill the gap: レストラン ＿ {食|た}べた。 ((I) ate at a restaurant.)
- で *
- に
- を
why: Eating is an action that happens at the restaurant, so the place takes で.

? choice particle-de
q: Fill the gap: {部屋|へや} ＿ いる。 ((I'm) in the room.)
- で
- に *
why: いる is about existing somewhere, so the place takes に.

? choice particle-de
q: What does バスで{行|い}く mean?
- Go by bus *
- Go to the bus
- Go on the bus's behalf
why: で marks the means: by bus.

? match particle-de
q: Match each sentence to its meaning.
- {図書館|としょかん}で{読|よ}む = read at the library
- {図書館|としょかん}に{行|い}く = go to the library
- {図書館|としょかん}にいる = be at the library
why: で for where an action happens, に for a destination or where something is.
:::
