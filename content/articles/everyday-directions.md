People in Japan are usually happy to help with directions. Start politely, ask simply, and listen for a few key words.

## Starting the conversation

::: example
ja: すみません。
en: Excuse me.
note: The same word also means "sorry". Here it gets someone's attention.
:::

## The question

::: breakdown
ja: {駅|えき} は どこ です か。
en: Where is the station?
- {駅|えき}は = as for the station
- どこ = where
- ですか = is it? (polite question)
:::

Swap {駅|えき} for any place: トイレ, {銀行|ぎんこう}, {郵便局|ゆうびんきょく}.

## Words to listen for

::: vocab
- まっすぐ | straight on | adverb
- {右|みぎ} | right | noun
- {左|ひだり} | left | noun
- {角|かど} | corner | noun
- {信号|しんごう} | traffic light | noun
- {近|ちか}い | near | い-adjective
- {遠|とお}い | far | い-adjective
:::

::: dialogue
A: すみません、{駅|えき} は どこ です か。 // Excuse me, where is the station?
B: まっすぐ {行|い}って、{信号|しんごう} を {右|みぎ} です。 // Go straight, then right at the lights.
A: {遠|とお}い です か。 // Is it far?
B: いいえ、{近|ちか}い です よ。 // No, it's close.
A: ありがとう ございます。 // Thank you very much.
:::

::: callout
title: When you are lost
{地図|ちず} is a map. Hold up your phone and ask ここ は どこ です か (Where is this?). Pointing at the map does the rest.
:::
