Japanese has a word, しゅん, for the moment a food is at its best. Shops, restaurants and even convenience stores change their menus to match.

## Spring

Cherry blossom season brings さくら-flavoured sweets, pink and lightly salty. Fresh たけのこ (bamboo shoots) appear in markets.

## Summer

Cold noodles like そうめん are served on ice. すいか (watermelon) is a summer treat, and so is かきごおり, shaved ice with syrup.

## Autumn

Autumn is the season of appetite. くり (chestnuts), さつまいも (sweet potatoes) and the fish さんま all reach their best.

## Winter

なべ, a pot of vegetables, tofu and meat or fish cooked at the table, is shared by family and friends. People also eat みかん (mandarin oranges) under the heated table.

::: vocab
- しゅん | in season | noun
- さくら | cherry blossom | spring
- すいか | watermelon | summer
- くり | chestnut | autumn
- なべ | hot pot | winter
- みかん | mandarin orange | winter
:::
